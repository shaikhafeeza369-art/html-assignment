Introduction

TechFest 2026 is the annual technology festival of our college. It brings together students, developers, innovators and technology enthusiasts to celebrate creativity and technical knowledge.

The festival provides students with an opportunity to demonstrate their skills through technical competitions, project exhibitions, workshops and interactive sessions.

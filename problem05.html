Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is made up of hydrogen and oxygen. Its chemical formula is H2O.

Carbon dioxide has the formula CO2 and is an important gas involved in the carbon cycle.

Oxygen gas is represented as O2, while hydrogen gas is represented as H2.

Common salt has the chemical formula NaCl. Another important compound is calcium carbonate, represented as CaCO3.

Atoms and molecules are the basic building blocks studied in chemistry.

Physics

Speed is the distance travelled by an object per unit of time. The formula for speed is:

Speed = Distance / Time

The SI unit of area is m2, while the SI unit of volume is m3.

The speed of light in vacuum is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Force is an important concept in physics. The basic formula for force is F = ma.

Biology

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.

Water is essential for living organisms and is represented by H2O.

Oxygen, O2, is required by many living organisms for respiration.

Carbon dioxide, CO2, is used by green plants during photosynthesis.

Welcome to Financial Education

Learn Before You Invest

Financial education helps you understand how to manage money, save for the future and make responsible financial decisions.

Before choosing a financial product, always understand the interest rate, charges, repayment period and other important conditions.

Important: Read all terms and conditions carefully before making financial decisions.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is useful for keeping money safely while earning interest.

Current Account

Current accounts are mainly designed for businesses and frequent transactions.

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows you to keep money for a fixed period and earn interest.

Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a part of your income regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Compare interest rates before taking a loan.
  • Maintain an emergency fund.
  • Pay your bills and EMIs on time.
  • Protect your banking passwords and OTPs.
  • Read financial documents carefully.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank and account type.
  2. Fill out the account-opening application.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Complete identity verification.
  5. Deposit the required amount, if applicable.
  6. Receive your account details and banking facilities.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or investment is held.

Understanding Loans

Home Loan

A home loan helps eligible customers finance the purchase or construction of a house.

Education Loan

An education loan can help eligible students meet approved education expenses.

Personal Loan

A personal loan may be used for eligible personal expenses and is usually repaid through EMIs.

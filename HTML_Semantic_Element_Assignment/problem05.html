Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a major career and education event designed for students, graduates and job seekers. It provides opportunities to meet leading organizations, explore courses and understand different career paths.

The event will include career counselling, internships, apprenticeships, placement opportunities and scholarship guidance. Our objective is to help participants make informed career decisions.

Objectives

  • Connect students with reputed organizations.
  • Provide information about careers and higher education.
  • Offer internship and placement opportunities.
  • Guide students about scholarships and apprenticeships.
  • Develop interview and communication skills.

Preparation Guide

Important Preparation Tips

  • Prepare a professional resume.
  • Research the organizations before attending.
  • Practice common interview questions.
  • Wear suitable formal or professional clothing.
  • Carry copies of your resume and certificates.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Check the event schedule.
  3. Visit organization stalls.
  4. Attend career guidance sessions.
  5. Participate in interviews and workshops.
  6. Connect with recruiters and mentors.

Career Information & FAQs

Career Fair

A career fair is an event where employers and job seekers meet to discuss career opportunities.

Internship

An internship gives students practical experience in a professional environment.

Placement

Placement refers to opportunities provided by organizations for suitable candidates to join their workforce.

Scholarship

A scholarship is financial support provided to eligible students for education.

Apprenticeship

An apprenticeship combines practical training with learning in a particular profession.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers and career explorers can attend.

Is registration required?

Yes. Participants should complete the registration form before attending.

What should I bring?

Bring your resume, identification documents and relevant certificates.

Will internships be available?

Yes, several participating organizations will provide internship information.

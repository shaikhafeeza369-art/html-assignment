About the University

GLA University supports students through quality education, practical learning, and modern technology. Learning, innovation, and discipline are important values of the university.

Academic Programmes

The university offers courses in Engineering, Computer Science, Management, Pharmacy, and many other fields. The B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) programme helps students learn Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Students may also learn scientific concepts such as H2O, CO2, and areas measured in m2.

Campus Facilities

The campus includes a library, computer laboratories, classrooms, sports facilities, hostel accommodation, and a cafeteria. All students must use campus facilities responsibly.

Important Notices

Attendance in classes is compulsory.
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to appear for examinations.

Assignment submissions should be completed before the teacher's deadline. Late submissions may receive reduced marks.

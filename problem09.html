Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must treat teachers, staff members, and classmates with respect.
Respectful behaviour creates a safe and friendly learning environment.

Students should keep classrooms clean and take care of school property.

Attendance

Students must attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Students with attendance below 75% may not be allowed to take final examinations.

Examination

Cheating, copying, or using unfair means during an examination is strictly prohibited.

Students should bring their own pens, pencils, and required materials to the examination hall.

Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must treat teachers, staff members, and classmates with respect.
Respectful behaviour creates a safe and friendly learning environment.

Students should keep classrooms clean and take care of school property.

Attendance

Students must attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Students with attendance below 75% may not be allowed to take final examinations.

Examination

Cheating, copying, or using unfair means during an examination is strictly prohibited.

Students should bring their own pens, pencils, and required materials to the examination hall.

Discipline

Students must follow school rules during class, breaks, and school events.
Repeated misconduct may lead to a meeting with parents or guardians.

Mobile phones should remain switched off during class unless a teacher gives permission.

New Technology Lab Opens at GLA University

Students Get a New Opportunity to Explore Artificial Intelligence

GLA University has opened a new technology laboratory to provide students with practical learning opportunities. The laboratory is designed to help students understand modern technologies and work on innovative projects.

The new facility includes resources for programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and other areas of computer science. The laboratory will be available for students to use for academic projects and technical activities.

The laboratory was officially opened on 30 August 2026 at the university campus. Faculty members encouraged students to use the facility responsibly and develop practical solutions to real-world problems.

Students expressed their excitement about the new facility. This initiative can help students connect classroom knowledge with practical experience. It is expected to encourage more students to participate in technical projects and competitions.

Editor's Note

The introduction of new learning facilities can give students more opportunities to develop technical skills. Students are encouraged to make effective use of such resources and continue learning new technologies.
